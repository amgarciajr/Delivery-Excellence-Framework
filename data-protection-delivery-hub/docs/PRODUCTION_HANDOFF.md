# Production handoff plan

## P0: governed delivery experience

- Approve lifecycle stage templates and definitions of done.
- Map tasks to engagements, workstreams, requirements, evidence, risks, decisions, and deliverables.
- Configure role-based transitions and human approval boundaries.
- Enable Dataverse audit, retention, and restricted engagement teams.

## P1: measurable practice improvement

- Define metric calculation owners and source records.
- Load historical metric periods and validate trend calculations.
- Implement sanitized lessons-learned and reusable-asset publication workflow.
- Track adoption, rework reduction, recurring blockers, and outcome realization.

## P2: production enablement

- Configure Dataverse and SharePoint adapters through environment variables and connection references.
- Complete managed solution ALM, solution checker, dependency review, backup, rollback, monitoring, and support runbook.
- Execute functional, role, accessibility, responsive, recovery, performance, and regression tests.
- Obtain product, technical, support, data, and approval ownership sign-off.

The current application remains synthetic/local until these controls are approved and verified.

Detailed implementation and cutover guidance is maintained in `docs/AVANADE_INTEGRATION_GUIDE.md`.
