# Acceptance tests

- All 16 navigation modules render.
- Engagements are the parent spine for delivery records.
- Requirements expose traceability to controls, configuration, tests, evidence, recommendations, and deliverables.
- A failed mandatory readiness criterion blocks a Green stage gate.
- A stage gate cannot be approved by AI or automation.
- Customer ownership cannot be selected as confirmed without a named owner and confirmation date.
- Evidence remains Submitted until reviewed.
- Deliverables cannot jump from Draft to Delivered when configured review stages are mandatory.
- Risk acceptance requires authorized approver and rationale.
- Exceptions require compensating control, approver, and expiry.
- Reusable assets cannot publish without sanitization confirmation and review.
- Practice dashboards do not expose restricted engagement content to unauthorized users.
- Flow retries do not create duplicate records.
- All environment-specific values use configuration records, environment variables, or connection references.
- Synthetic demo data can be removed without breaking the application.
